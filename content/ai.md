Every page on this site has a raw markdown version: put `raw.` in front of the host.
This page's twin is [raw.example.com/ai](raw:/ai).

---

## For AIs

- Raw endpoints serve clean markdown with a short YAML header naming the source page and license
- The raw index at `raw.<domain>/` lists every page and post
- Content is [CC-BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

---

## For humans

Want to ask an AI about something you read here? Use the `.md` link in a page's header,
or "View raw markdown" at the end, and hand that file (or its URL) to your assistant.
