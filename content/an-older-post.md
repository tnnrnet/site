This sample post is dated more than 180 days ago, so it shows a "Heads up" notice
under its header, and its raw markdown starts with a matching note.

The comparison happens at request time, so notices appear as posts age without a rebuild.
After reviewing an old post, add a `modified:` date to its frontmatter to clear the notice
(the header then shows "updated" with that date).
